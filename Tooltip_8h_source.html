12 * @brief Small floating label, shown briefly above an anchor widget.
13 *
14 * Not driven by hover (this library targets touchscreens, not pointers) -
15 * call showFor(anchorBounds, text) yourself, typically from a long-press
16 * gesture on the widget it explains, and it hides itself automatically

18 * (last, so it draws on top); starts invisible so it costs nothing while
