Detailed Description

The WiFi of a device as WifiLoginPage uses it: scan, connect and the stored credentials. All calls must return quickly - the scan and the connection run in the background and are polled.

Implementations: Esp32Wifi (TinyMaterialDesign/Wifi/WifiEsp32.h, not included by TinyMaterialDesign.h) and SimulatedWifi (desktop, tests); for another board derive from this class.
